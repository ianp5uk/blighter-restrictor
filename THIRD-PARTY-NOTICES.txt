Blighter Restrictor - third-party software notices.
Purpose: lists the software by other people that is shipped inside the
Blighter Restrictor installers and packages, with its licence and where its
source code can be obtained.  The full licence texts are in the "licenses"
folder; the exact licence files of each Python package in the Windows parent
app are in its "third-party-licenses" folder.
